A Two-Sided Inflation Squeeze Before Decision Day

UK CPI returned to 3.1%, Japan's energy bill widened its trade deficit, and oil above $100 kept moving through household costs, terms of trade and asset discount rates.

Main theme · Editorial view

Confirmed: August UK CPI was 3.1%, Japanese imports rose 28% year over year and Brent remained near $108. The Federal Reserve statement was not yet available.

View: The key is not guessing one hike, but testing whether the energy shock is spreading into broader prices, trade balances and margins. Persistent transmission would make a quick dovish turn difficult even without repeated tightening.
