Today’s line

Slower growth and still-high inflation meet fresh evidence that AI spending can produce earnings. The key question is shifting from demand to whether demand can cover capex, depreciation, and cash flow.

1. GDP slows to 1.5%; PCE falls to 3.7%

Fact: Q2 real GDP grew at a 1.5% annualized rate, below Q1’s 2.1%; June PCE was 3.7% y/y, down from 4.1% in May.

Market impact (inference): Cooling growth with inflation above target may limit easing and keep growth stocks data-sensitive.

Counter-risk: GDP will be revised; one soft quarter is not recession.

AP · BEA GDP · BEA PCE

2. Fed holds 3.50%–3.75%; three dissenters favor a hike

Fact: The July 29 FOMC voted 9–3 to hold, with three members preferring a 25bp hike; the statement said inflation remained above 2%.

Market impact (inference): Two-year yields, the dollar, and high-duration equities remain exposed to repricing.

Counter-risk: Dissent is not a commitment; cooler data could revive easing expectations.

Fed · AP

3. Apple beats

Fact: Fiscal Q3 revenue was $109.4bn and EPS $2.02, with revenue up 16%; next-quarter revenue guidance is 9%–11% y/y, with FX and supply constraints flagged.

Market impact (inference): Apple supports the consumer-hardware and services earnings case.

Counter-risk: Refunds, FX, and memory costs may pressure later margins.

AP · Axios

4. Amazon adds $20bn to AI-heavy capex

Fact: Q2 sales and profit were strong, with AWS a key driver; Amazon added $20bn to 2026 technology capex, mostly AI.

Market impact (inference): Cloud demand can validate AI infrastructure revenue, but depreciation, FCF, and data-center execution face scrutiny.

Counter-risk: Capex can arrive before revenue; power, chips, and customer concentration can bottleneck growth.

AP · Amazon IR

5. Microsoft leads an AI-stock rebound

Fact: AP described one of Microsoft’s strongest one-day gains since 2008; S&P +1.7%, Dow +613.92, Nasdaq +679.24. Microsoft reported about $90bn revenue and $35.8bn net income.

Market impact (inference): Capital favors companies converting AI spend into cloud revenue and profit.

Counter-risk: If capex outruns FCF or customer returns, the rebound can reverse.

AP · Axios
