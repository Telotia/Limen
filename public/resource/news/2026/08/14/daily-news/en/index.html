01Macro | Headline PPI cooled, but services costs did not disappear

Fact
Final-demand PPI was unchanged monthly and rose 4.7% annually. Goods fell 0.7%, energy 3.1% and gasoline 5.7%; services rose 0.2%. Excluding food, energy and trade services, PPI rose 0.4% monthly and 4.7% annually.

Market impact · Inference
Headline relief helps bonds and long-duration equities, but services costs imply uneven margin improvement.

Counter-risk · View
July's energy decline predates August oil volatility, while the narrow core measure remains hot enough to make “inflation solved” premature.

Sources: Bureau of Labor Statistics · PPI database · AP markets report · Federal Reserve

02Rates and FX | Hike odds fell to about 35%, but oil and the yen can rewrite the path

Fact
Implied September hike odds fell to about 35% from roughly 50% two days earlier, and the 10-year yield declined to 4.65% from 4.68%. USD/JPY was 159.16 and EUR/USD 1.1556; Brent rebounded about 1% to $87.96 after Thursday's 2.1% decline.

Market impact · Inference
The upside tail for front-end rates narrowed, while the long end still carries energy, fiscal and term-premium risk. Yen levels near 160 increase intervention risk.

Counter-risk · View
Strong retail sales, higher inflation expectations or shipping disruption could quickly reverse the decline in yields and the dollar.

Sources: AP, August 13 · AP, August 14 · CME FedWatch · U.S. Energy Information Administration

03Equities | U.S. records coexist with Asian divergence

Fact
The S&P 500 rose 0.7% to 7,798.99, the Nasdaq added 0.8% to 26,803.03 and the Dow gained 0.1%. The Nikkei 225 rose 0.6% and the Kospi 2.4%; the Hang Seng fell 1.1%, Shanghai was nearly flat and Taiwan lost 0.5%.

Market impact · Inference
Disinflation and AI exposure support the United States, Japan and South Korea, but the move is not globally synchronized. Consumption data now matter for earnings breadth.

Counter-risk · View
Light turnover and index concentration can amplify gains; weak consumption could turn lower yields into an earnings warning.

Sources: AP global markets · AP U.S. close · Japan Exchange Group · Korea Exchange

04Consumption and housing | Demand faces a test; mortgage relief is limited

Fact
Retail sales are due at 08:30 ET and preliminary consumer sentiment at 10:00 ET. June retail sales rose 0.2%, or 0.7% excluding gasoline stations. The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate eased to 6.67% from 6.69%, still above 6.58% a year ago.

Market impact · Inference
Resilient spending with stable inflation expectations would preserve a soft-landing mix. A small mortgage-rate decline offers only marginal help to homebuilders and residential REITs.

Counter-risk · View
Retail sales are nominal and distorted by fuel and promotions; a two-basis-point mortgage decline does not confirm a housing rebound.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau · AP retail sales · University of Michigan · Freddie Mac · AP mortgage rates

05Sectors | Real estate gained while networking fell

Fact
AvalonBay Communities gained 2.3% and D.R. Horton 2.8%. Cisco Systems fell 8.4% despite above-forecast quarterly profit and revenue. In the prior quarter, revenue had risen 12% to $15.8 billion, while GAAP product gross margin fell to 61.9% from 64.4%.

Market impact · Inference
Markets rewarded rate beneficiaries and penalized margin pressure. AI-related revenue still has to become profit.

Counter-risk · View
One-day prices also reflect valuation and positioning; a rebound in yields could reverse the real-estate rotation.

Sources: AP market reaction · Cisco official results · Cisco earnings materials · Freddie Mac

06Company | Applied Materials raised guidance; pre-market trading tests the good-news threshold

Fact
Revenue was $9.12 billion, up 25%, with non-GAAP EPS of $3.50. Next-quarter guidance centered on $10.25 billion of revenue and $4.02 of non-GAAP EPS. Shares traded lower before the open; regular trading had not begun, so no intraday percentage is used.

Market impact · Inference
Advanced logic, DRAM and advanced-packaging demand remain validated, but valuation requires persistent upside surprises. Margins, cash flow and 2027 growth matter more now.

Counter-risk · View
Pre-market liquidity is thin and guidance depends on delivery timing; the initial move is not a final verdict.

Sources: Applied Materials official results · Quarterly call · Investor materials · Earnings calendar and expectations context
