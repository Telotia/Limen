Fed minutes: Tightening may be needed if inflation fails to decline

FactThe July meeting voted 9–3 to hold the federal funds target at 3.50%–3.75%. Many participants said tightening would likely be necessary if inflation did not decline, with inflation risks tilted upward.

Market impact · InferenceDownside in front-end yields and the dollar may be limited, while growth stocks rely more on delivered earnings. Continued labor softness could make the three-week-old minutes look stale.

Counter-riskRecent inflation cooling and weak July employment could delay a hike; renewed energy or services inflation would expose underpriced hawkish risk.

Fed statement · Fed minutes · AP · Axios

2

Treasury expands long-bond buybacks and cools the term premium

FactLiquidity-support buybacks in 10–20-year and 20–30-year nominal sectors will at least double from September 9 through November 4. The 10-year yield fell as low as 4.64% from 4.71%; the 30-year reached 5.18% from 5.28%.

Market impact · InferenceBetter liquidity cushions duration assets, housing and small caps, but does not directly change deficits, inflation or net financing needs.

Counter-riskThe scale is small relative to the Treasury market. Fiscal supply, oil and AI corporate issuance could push yields up again. Buybacks are not quantitative easing.

AP bond analysis · AP market reaction · Axios · Treasury FAQ

3

China holds LPRs for a fifteenth month

FactThe one-year LPR stayed at 3.00% and the five-year-and-over rate at 3.50%, matching consensus. Maturing operations produced a net RMB327.4 billion liquidity drain.

Market impact · InferenceSteady rates protect bank margins and currency stability, but leave property and private demand without a new broad-based rate impulse.

Counter-riskEffective loan rates, property rules and fiscal tools can still loosen. Persistent demand weakness could trigger a reserve-ratio or policy-rate cut.

LPR history · Reuters · Rate fix report · Liquidity report

4

U.S. stocks snap a three-day slide, led by rate relief

FactThe S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq each gained about 0.2%; the Russell 2000 rose 0.5%. Through Wednesday, the S&P and Nasdaq were still down roughly 1.0% and 1.5% for the week.

Market impact · InferenceSmall-cap leadership alongside lower long yields points to discount rates as the immediate driver. Stable yields could broaden leadership into retail, industrials and domestic demand.

Counter-riskA 0.2% daily gain is not a trend, and better earnings may not fully offset a higher risk-free rate.

AP index close · AP market drivers · Reuters on rates and FX

5

Retail earnings become a consumer stress test

FactResults at Target and Estée Lauder supported Wednesday's market. Walmart and Alibaba scheduled releases before today's U.S. open. Walmart's prior guidance called for 4%–5% constant-currency sales growth and adjusted EPS of $0.72–$0.74. Complete current-quarter results were not verifiable by the cutoff.

Market impact · InferenceWalmart tests U.S. traffic and online profitability; Alibaba tests Chinese consumption and returns on AI and cloud investment.

Counter-riskCurrency, one-offs and guidance language can magnify premarket moves. Official releases must replace estimates once available.

Walmart event · Walmart guidance · Alibaba notice · AP

6

Unitree closes 460% above its IPO price

FactThe RMB150.80 offering raised about RMB6.1 billion. Shares traded up as much as 629% and closed at RMB845, a 460% gain. Retail demand exceeded the available tranche more than 8,000 times.

Market impact · InferenceThe listing creates a public valuation anchor for robotics components and automation, but the gain mainly reflects scarcity, limited float and thematic flows.

Counter-riskThe issue price already represented roughly 219 times 2025 earnings. Shipments do not guarantee margins or free cash flow; manufacturing, use-case and lockup risks remain.

AP · Reuters listing preview · Reuters valuation context · Reuters industry view · Shanghai Stock Exchange

7

08:30 ET labor and manufacturing data set today's direction

FactInitial jobless claims and the Philadelphia Fed's August survey are due at 08:30 ET. This brief predates the releases and reports no actual values. July's headline factory index was 41.4 and employment index 10.0.

Market impact · InferenceHigher claims plus cooling factories favor bonds; low claims plus firm price components return attention to tightening risk.

Counter-riskWeekly claims are noisy and a regional survey is not national production. Conflicting signals could leave markets waiting for Friday's PMIs.
