Love of Winter · George Wesley Bellows · 1914 · Oil on canvas

Cold ice. A warm hand.

Two joined hands make a small pocket of warmth inside a vast winter.

Let cold spread. Bring warmth close.

COMPOSITION

Dark hills above, bright ice below. Joined hands link two warm shapes.

COLOR

A blue-green field; red-orange accents. Near-complementary hues sharpen the warmth.

LIGHT

Snow and pale clothing echo each other. Dark coats give the brightness an edge.
