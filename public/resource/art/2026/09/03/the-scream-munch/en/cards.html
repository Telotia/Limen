The rails rush toward the upper left while two walkers recede. The frontal figure stops. Mouth, face, clouds, and shoreline repeat one oval rhythm, joining body and nature into a psychic waveform.

TEMPERA + CRAYON · CARDBOARD · DIRECTIONAL MARKS

Rails thrust backward, sky and water undulate laterally, and the figure contracts inward. Line becomes psychological velocity; looping bands, dry texture, and exposed ground keep the image under immediate pressure.

Fact: Edvard Munch (1863–1944) was a Norwegian artist and a major precursor of Expressionism. Museum research traces the motif to an experience he wrote down in 1892. This 1893 work is the earlier painted version; pastel and lithographic versions followed.

Others keep walking. The world loses its acoustic wall.

Interpretation: Hands pressed to the ears leave the source unresolved. Perhaps this is not someone producing the scream, but someone receiving it—anxiety translated from expression into space.
