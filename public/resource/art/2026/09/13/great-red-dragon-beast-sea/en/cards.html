When a body

01

Two triangles.
One forbidden opening.

Gate form · Inverted triangle · Muted near-complements
Form

Wings, torso, and legs build the outer gate. The sea beast forms a counter-triangle, visible only through the central slit.

Color

Mauve flesh faces verdigris blue. Both sides lose saturation, turning contrast into a faded alarm.

Light

Chest and faces emerge like rubbed relief. The cooler, brighter figure below is still pushed under the surface.

02

Line pins it down.
Watercolor keeps it mutating.

Graphite structure · Ink contour · Brown wash · Starred wings

Graphite sets the anatomy; ink fixes horns, faces, and fingers. Watercolor disperses through wings, sea, and shadow. The contour is certain while the creature keeps forming.

Fact: Made c. 1805 for the Bible for Thomas Butts series. William Blake was a poet, painter, and printmaker trained as an engraver.

Interpretation: Seven faces are strange.
Its composure is terrifying.

The dragon does not attack; it owns the frame. Yet every face looks elsewhere. Absolute power appears whole while already divided within.

A practical formula
for visual pressure

Poster · Portrait · Layout · Palette
  1. Frame: Let the primary shape fill 82–90% of the height and touch three edges; build a wide-top, narrow-waist gate.
  2. Reveal: Show only 25–35% of the second figure inside one narrow negative shape. Fragments amplify scale.
  3. Color: #2C2726 42% + #8B7770 24% + #547C7C 18% + #D8C3A0 11% + warm accents 5%.
  4. Light: Hold the ground 1.5–2 stops darker. Give peak brightness only to the face, chest, or key message.
