CENTRAL AXIS · RADIAL REPEAT · ANALOGOUS COLOR
Composition

A wide cone presses onto a round font. Beads dissolve the heavy rim; scrolling feet distribute weight outward.

Color

Bronze-black becomes amber and milk yellow. A 2% peacock teal corrects the orange warmth.

Light

Metal ribs cut the glow; glass releases it. Brightness lives inside while the outline stays near-black.

Opulence

LEADED PANES · BLOWN FONT · PATINATED BRONZE

Leaded panes form the dome; the blown Favrile font keeps its cloudy striations. Dark bronze carries outline and rhythm. Glass carries hue, depth, and interior glow.

Fact: dated about 1895–98, this is among the workshop’s earliest lamps. The collar includes Islamic-inspired pattern. No individual designer is listed.

Interpretation: beads describe gravity;
radial ribs describe light spreading.

Every ornament serves one task: making light visible. Darkness is not defeated; it becomes amber’s boundary.
