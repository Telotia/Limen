She looks away—

Satin black, cool skin, and a public appearance built on withheld eye contact.

TORSION · DOUBLE TRIANGLE · MUTED WARM–COOL · MATERIAL AS COLOR
Composition

Face right, torso forward, left arm descending. Empty wall releases the gaze; the small table anchors the opposite corner.

Color

Warm smoked umber supports cool ivory skin. Blue-gray, charcoal, and brown value shifts turn black into satin.

Light

The eyes withdraw; shoulders become the brightest zone. A dark backdrop and a few cool reflections recover the dress's volume.

One cut at distance,

OIL ON CANVAS · PRECISE CONTOUR · WET REFLECTION · 1884 PARIS SALON

Face and silhouette tighten; skirt, table, and wall dissolve. Long wet strokes of gray-black produce satin, while pink, gray, and blue-green keep the skin cool.

Fact: Sargent pursued the portrait of Virginie Gautreau. After its hostile 1884 Salon reception, he repainted the jeweled strap that had originally slipped from one shoulder.

Interpretation: she is displayed—
and controls the display.

The dress emphasizes the body; the profile refuses to please. The raised strap restores decorum while ensuring we remember that it once fell.

LAYOUT · PALETTE · STYLING · PORTRAIT
  1. Layout: Fill 75–85% of frame height, just left of center. Keep 25–30% air on the gaze side; anchor the opposite lower corner.
  2. Palette: #171719 38% + #4C4237 30% + #D9C7B4 20% + #8A584C 7% + #B69A62 5%.
  3. Styling: 80% black + 15% cool ivory + 5% aged gold. Mix matte and satin black; keep jewelry to one point.
  4. Portrait: Body to camera, face away 60–80°. Use broad soft light on the neck; hold the backdrop 1.5–2 stops darker.
