BROKEN EDGE · CENTRAL HINGE · MUTED COMPLEMENTS

We see the basket more frontally, the plate from above, the bottle near eye level. The left falls; the right lifts. The dark bottle hinges both forces together.

Blue-gray and mist white set the field. Rust and olive form a muted complementary pair. The near-black bottle and pale cloth anchor value; warm–cool contrast makes the fruit glow.

Not an error.
Time enters the picture.

CARBON DRAWING · COLOR PATCHES · VARIABLE TOUCH

Apples are not blended into perfect spheres. Short strokes of yellow, red, green, and cool blue assemble them. Fruit and cloth feel sculptural; peripheral edges remain sketch-like.

FACT: Museum infrared imaging shows that the broken table edge and tilted bottle were planned in the underdrawing, then revised. Painted c. 1893, this is among the few works Cezanne signed and appeared in his first solo exhibition in 1895.

Eyes travel. Memory folds one second into the next.

INTERPRETATION: The tilts preserve the time of looking. Ordinary apples become monumental without a story—only through sustained attention.
