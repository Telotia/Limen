No camera.
Compose with light.

Contact distance · exposure · opacity · movement

A hand, brush, and translucent objects meet sensitised paper. Touching, hovering, sliding, and rotating yield hard edges, soft edges, echoes, and density shifts.

Fact: Made in 1926, this gelatin silver print measures 23.9 × 17.9 cm. Moholy-Nagy taught at the Bauhaus and treated photography, design, material, and light as one experimental field.

Interpretation: set the conditions,
then let light decide.

We know the hand but cannot tell whether it reaches, refuses, or touches. The body splits into several durations—intimate and strange.

Take darkroom logic
into your frame

Photography · layout · dress
  1. Frame: 70% dark negative space + 20% translucent grey + 10% hard highlight; use a 25°–35° diagonal.
  2. Darkroom: Hover film, mesh, leaves, or a hand over paper; make three short exposures at different heights. Follow chemical safety rules.
  3. Layout: one thick diagonal + one family of fine parallel lines; let scale contrast replace decoration.
  4. Dress: charcoal 65% + fog grey 25% + bone 10%; add one translucent outer layer.
