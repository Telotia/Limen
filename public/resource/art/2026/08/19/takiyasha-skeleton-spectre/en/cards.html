The spectre crosses seams.

Horizontal mass · X-field · Black cavities

The skeleton locks three vertical sheets into one stage: skull bearing down, spine arcing right, hand reaching below. Human action is compressed into the lower 35%; unequal scale creates pressure first.

A low-chroma split complementary: warm beige and red against faded indigo. Three dark wells—sockets, nose, mouth—replace modelled shadow.

02

Anatomy carved
into visual rhythm

Key block · Registered colour · Line density

The key block holds the contour; separate blocks layer restrained colour. Lines tighten at teeth, joints, and sockets, then relax along long bones. The supernatural becomes a credible skeleton.

Fact: Utagawa Kuniyoshi (1797–1861) was a major late-Edo print designer known for warriors, bizarre invention, and comedy. This triptych dates to 1845–46.

Practical Notes · Source04 / 04
03

Make scale immense
with limited colour

A reusable palette and layout
  1. Palette: paper beige #D8C6A0 65% + charcoal 18% + faded indigo 10% + iron red 5% + old gold 2%.
  2. Layout: span one form across 2–3 columns; compress people into the lower 35%; counter one dominant diagonal.
  3. Suspense: skip gore. Make sockets, doorways, or shadows the largest black areas; preserve the pale contour.
  4. Outfit: beige outerwear 60% + charcoal 25% + blue-grey 12% + dark red accessory 3%.
