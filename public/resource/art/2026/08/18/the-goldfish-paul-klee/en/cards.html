01

One fish takes the center.
Seven retreat to the edge.

Horizontal thrust · Orbital cast · Black buffer

The main fish sits just below center and pushes right. Seven smaller bodies gather along the perimeter. A broad zone of negative space keeps the focal contour clean.

An 80% blue-black field surrounds 20% gold-red warmth. Near complements plus value contrast trigger simultaneous contrast—and the fish appears to glow.

Watercolor transparency.
Oil-paint weight.

Mixed media on paper · Sign-like marks · Bauhaus years

Collection records identify oil and watercolor on paper mounted on board. Washes bloom; oil covers. Dots, curls, short lines, and zigzags turn fish, plants, and currents into visual grammar.

Fact: Klee (1879–1940) taught at the Bauhaus from 1921. Made in 1925, this work is held by Hamburger Kunsthalle.

Build an inner glow
without a white halo

A reusable palette and composition
  1. Palette: blue-black #0B0C12 55% + ultramarine 25% + ochre gold 10% + vermilion 7% + bright yellow 3%.
  2. Glow: cool dark field → ochre edge → yellow-orange core → very little bright yellow.
  3. Layout: one horizontal form at about 55% width, just below center; 5–7 small edge forms and a clean buffer.
  4. Wear it: ink navy 70% + ultramarine 20% + ochre 8% + vermilion 2%; keep gold to one accessory.
