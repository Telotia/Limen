SCALE PERSPECTIVE · ADVANCING HEAT · AFTERIMAGE

Large, medium, small: three numerals recede along the center. Diagonals, machine facets, and arcs throw the eye forward again. The engine retreats while sensation hits head-on.

OIL · GRAPHITE · INK · GOLD LEAF

Graphite builds the exact armature, oil fills clean planes, ink tightens edges, and gold leaf changes with the viewing angle. Cubist fragments, Futurist vectors, and Precisionist hard edges converge.

Fact: Charles Demuth (1883–1935) drew on his friend William Carlos Williams’s poem The Great Figure for this faceless “poster portrait,” one of eight made from 1924 to 1929.

What remains is not the whole street: one number, one flare, one urgent sound.

A FORMULA TO BORROW
  1. Palette: fire-engine red #B92B1F 45% + ochre gold #D59A18 30% + blue-black #182231 15% + steel and cream 5% each.
  2. Hierarchy: repeat one symbol three times at roughly 1 : 0.48 : 0.20.
  3. Motion: one level anchor + two or three diagonal systems + one field-crossing arc.
  4. Wear it: charcoal outer layer + brick-red top + mustard-gold accessory; keep cream near the face.
