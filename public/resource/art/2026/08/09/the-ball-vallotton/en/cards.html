01

A god’s-eye view with no answer

A field of distance

Shadow, grass, and path interlock like cut-paper wedges. The child enters from below; cast shadow, body, and red ball form a diagonal action line. Two distant figures complete a narrow triangle and steady the vast negative space.

Ochre and moss form a muted analogous chord. Warm white lifts the child by value. Less than 1% vermilion complements the green—and becomes the loudest note.

02

A woodcut blade inside an oil painting

Flat form · open story

Vallotton made his name in woodcut and moved close to the Nabis. Here brushwork does not perform: contours are decisive, shapes flat. A high viewpoint and sharp crop make oil behave like a color print.

Fact: painted in 1899 in oil on cardboard mounted on wood, 49.2 × 62 cm. The museum notes that figure scale implies depth while the space remains strangely flat and frontal.

We see far, yet cannot identify the two figures. Suspense lives inside that distance.

03

Let one small color move the frame

Layout and palette recipe
  1. Layout: 70% quiet field + 25% dark cutout + 5% bright subject.
  2. Palette: 55% ochre + 25% moss + 15% ink green + 4% warm white + 1% red.
  3. Outfit: moss jacket + oat trousers + warm-white top + one vermilion bag.
  4. Photo: shoot downward, place the figure low, point the shadow into motion.
