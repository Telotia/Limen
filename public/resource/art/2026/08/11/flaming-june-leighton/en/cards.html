Classical structure.

Academic drawing · Aesthetic surface

Leighton builds a credible figure, then separates skin, gauze, stone, and air with controlled oil layers. Repeated arcs turn anatomy into ornament.

Fact: Frederic Leighton (1830–1896) was a British painter and sculptor who served as president of the Royal Academy. Completed in 1895, the work is framed by its collection within Aestheticism.

The body need not perform. Colour strikes first; silence keeps us.
