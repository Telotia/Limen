One horizontal disciplines the vertical

Vertical rhythm, liquid light

Dense pleats draw the body into one long column. A pale belt makes a single horizontal pause; a small pool at the hem releases the strict descent.

02

Shape through material, not a rigid frame

Material intelligence

Fact: the Delphos type emerged around 1907 and was patented in 1909. Rectangular silk falls from the shoulders; fine pleats expand with the body, while glass beads weight the soft edges.

Mariano worked across painting, stage light, and textiles; Adèle was his creative partner. Records differ: this object is under Mariano, while another major museum names both for a comparable example.

Sculpture at rest, water in motion—the body enters, and the dress becomes complete.

My reading: freedom is hidden inside hundreds of disciplined, never quite identical lines.

03

Make liquid light wearable

A practical celadon formula
  1. 65% celadon #78999A: dress, shirt, or the largest tailored piece.
  2. 20% deep teal #456F73: replace black shoes or bag with a tonal dark.
  3. 10% mist silver #C8CECA + 5% aged gold #A99162: soften with a pale layer; warm with jewellery.
  4. Texture rule: one pleated or embossed hero piece; keep everything else matte.
