A home becomes
an unmarked ruler

Scale rhyme · Negative space · Value

A hand, slippers, a bed, a chair, and a doorway take turns as measuring devices. Similar rooms and changing ratios make growth visible without dates.

Paper ivory #E9E1D2, smoke grey #8D877D, and ink #2A2926 form a low-saturation analogous harmony. Diffused light becomes soft value; the darkest marks are saved for eyes, ears, and overlapping contours.

02

Photography says “happened.”
Ink says “remembered.”

Eight images · Frame compositing · Original sound

Eight discrete states per second are held for three frames in a 24 fps master. Bleeding edges let rooms withdraw; slight hesitation breaks four years into moments the film is reluctant to release.

Fact: Daily Art made the work from private family images, retaining subject count, age, markings, pose, gaze, physical contact, and principal scale.

My reading: growth does not leave the home; it rewrites the scale of everything inside it.

03

Bring warm paper
and dense ink home

A reusable palette and composition
  1. Palette: #E9E1D2 70% + #8D877D 20% + #2A2926 8% + #A9483B 2%.
  2. Composition: repeat one everyday measuring object; use the lower third and keep 35% open space.
  3. Light: choose diffused window light; keep highlights ivory and lift shadows until texture remains.
  4. Wardrobe/interior: oat 70% + charcoal 25% + vermilion 5%; place red only once.
