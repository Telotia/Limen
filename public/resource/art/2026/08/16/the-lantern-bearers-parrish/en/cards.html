Bodies rise;

Ascending diagonal · linked triangles · circular beats

Figures connect from lower left to upper right. Limbs and cords form linked triangles; horizontal steps stabilize the rush, while round lanterns create pauses.

Cobalt and gold are complementary: 70% blue-black atmosphere recedes, 20% muted violet mediates, and 10% golden light advances.

Like stained glass;
like a single stage cue

Pure pigment · varnish layers · print intelligence

The museum notes that Parrish layered pure pigment and varnish for brilliant depth. Crisp contours and broad color masses also keep the image legible when reduced in print.

Fact: Maxfield Parrish (1870–1966) was an American painter and illustrator. Completed in 1908, this work was later reproduced for the December 10, 1910 issue of Collier’s.

03

Bring this blue-gold night
into your own frame

A reusable palette and composition
  1. Palette: deep cobalt #163B67 55% + blue-black 15% + dusty violet 15% + rose gray 5% + lantern gold #F2B53F 10%.
  2. Glow: #FFE7A0 → #F2B53F → #B56B21; use one low-opacity amber halo.
  3. Composition: one rising axis + 3–5 body triangles; horizontal lines stabilize, circles pause.
  4. Outfit: 70% midnight blue + 20% dusty violet + 10% amber on earrings, shoes, or a small bag.
